Five-minute walkthrough

  1. Open the clinic view: 8 pets due for care, most valuable first. Biscuit carries a "Home check-ins: mobility down" tag.
  2. Open Priya's app. Under Costs, read Dr Lee's estimate line by line, tick the X-rays, tap Go ahead, then Send to clinic.
  3. In Last visit, tick an instruction and tap Book it for the recheck.
  4. Tap Something not right? Try "Vomiting, 1 to 3 days", then the paralysis-tick sign.
  5. Switch to Pip for the kitten's first 90 days, and Mango to see the bird told to call.
  6. Back in the clinic view, open Requests to see what Priya sent.
