Five-minute walkthrough

  1. Open the clinic dashboard: 8 care gaps ranked by value. Filter to Senior, send Biscuit's reminder.
  2. Open the owner agent: it raises Biscuit's overdue screening without being asked.
  3. Ask "Is my plan worth it?", then "Book his screening" and tap Send to clinic.
  4. Say "He's limping again" (drafts a note for Dr Lee), then "He's holding his leg up" (urgent: call the clinic).
  5. Log "31.2 kg", switch log sharing off in Data, log again. Back on the clinic dashboard, open Requests.
