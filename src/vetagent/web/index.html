Five-minute walkthrough

  1. Open the clinic view: 8 pets due for care, most valuable first. Biscuit carries a "Home check-ins: mobility down" tag from his owner's weekly check-ins.
  2. Open Biscuit's dashboard. The check-in tile shows mobility lower for 2 weeks: tap Ask for a check-up, then Send to clinic.
  3. Read Next 12 months of care: everything due this year, on his Senior plan and pay-as-you-go.
  4. In Parasite cover, see the gaps in his flea and tick cover, then tap Given today.
  5. Do this week's check-in. In Ask, try "He's holding his leg up" (urgent: call the clinic) and "Log weight 31.2 kg".
  6. Switch log sharing off under the shield icon: the clinic tag disappears. Back in the clinic view, open Requests.
